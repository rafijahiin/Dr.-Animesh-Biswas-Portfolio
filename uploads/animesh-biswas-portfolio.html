Dr. Animesh Biswas is a global health expert at UNFPA Bangladesh, specialising in Sexual and Reproductive Health in humanitarian emergencies. He pioneered the use of Cash and Voucher Assistance for SRH outcomes — a model now replicated across multiple countries — earning recognition as a profoundly dedicated and innovative programme lead.

A standing member of the WHO Global MPDSR Technical Working Group for over five years, Dr. Biswas has presented at international conferences, written case studies in WHO's 2024 global MPDSR report, and advised on global guidance for CVA in SRH programming. His field work spans from community-level maternal mortality reduction in Bangladesh to the Rohingya response in Cox's Bazar.
