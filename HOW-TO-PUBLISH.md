# How to publish — Dr. Animesh Biswas

This is a non-technical guide for posting writing, uploading photos, and managing the site.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **`https://animesh-biswas.netlify.app/admin/`** (or your live site URL + `/admin/`).
2. Sign in with the email and password you were sent in the invitation email.
3. The first time, you'll be asked to set a new password — choose something memorable.

You only need to log in once per device; the browser remembers you.

---

## 2. Writing a new Field Note (blog post)

1. From the admin sidebar, click **Field Notes (Blog) → New Field Note**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — the headline of the essay
   - **Published date** — when it should be dated
   - **Category badge** — pick from the dropdown (Maternal Health, MPDSR, etc.)
   - **Cover image** — *optional*. Click to upload. If empty, the site shows a tasteful placeholder.
   - **Summary** — 1–3 sentences. This is what appears on the homepage card.
   - **Featured** — toggle ON for the **one** post you want as the large card on the homepage.
   - **Draft** — leave ON while writing; turn OFF when ready to publish.
   - **Tags** — optional keywords
   - **Body** — write the essay here. You can:
     - Add headings (H2 / H3 from the toolbar)
     - Add **bold** and *italic*
     - Insert images by dragging into the editor
     - Add quotes, lists, and links
3. When ready, click **Save** then **Publish**. The site rebuilds itself within 1–2 minutes.

> Drafts are saved but not visible to the public. Use this to work on an essay over multiple sessions.

---

## 3. Uploading photos to the gallery

1. Click **Photo Gallery → New Photo**.
2. **Photo** — drag and drop the image, or click to upload.
3. **Category** — choose one:
   - **Field** — fieldwork, missions, on-assignment
   - **Conferences** — WHO, UNFPA, talks, panels
   - **Community** — outreach, training, community visits
   - **Personal** — couple / AniTap / off-duty
4. **Caption** — short description.
5. **Location, Date** — optional.
6. **Display order** — lower numbers appear first within their category. Leave blank and it goes to the end.
7. **Featured** — toggle ON for a *larger* tile in the grid.
8. Click **Save** then **Publish**.

The photo appears on the homepage in the **Field & Moments** chapter. Visitors can filter by category and click any photo to enlarge it.

---

## 4. Updating the numbers (Chapter 03)

1. Click **Site Settings → Numbers (Chapter 03)**.
2. Edit any of the four numbers and their suffixes (`+`, `K+`, etc.).
3. Click **Save → Publish**.

These animate up from 0 when a visitor scrolls to that section.

---

## 5. Featured publications

1. Click **Featured Publications → New Publication**.
2. Fill in title, year, venue, abstract, tags, and optional link.
3. Lower **Display order** = shown first.
4. Save → Publish.

Keep this list to **5–8 highlights**. The full 130+ list stays on ResearchGate.

---

## 6. The "Request bibliography" button

You have two options for this button at the end of the publications section:

- **Default:** opens a new email to you (`hello@animeshbiswas.com`) with subject "Bibliography request".
- **Upload a PDF:** go to **Site Settings → General Site Info → Bibliography PDF**, upload your full bibliography PDF. The button automatically switches to a direct download.

---

## 7. YouTube section

The site auto-fetches the latest videos from **@AniTapDoctorCouple**. To set this up:

1. Find your channel ID:
   - Visit `https://www.youtube.com/account_advanced` while logged in
   - Copy the long string starting with `UC...`
2. In the CMS, go to **Site Settings → YouTube Section**.
3. Paste it into **YouTube channel ID**.
4. Save → Publish.

Latest 3–4 videos will appear on the homepage in Chapter 07 — Diaries, updating automatically each time the site rebuilds.

**To pin/feature one video:** paste its full YouTube URL into the **Pinned video URL** field. It will show first with a "Featured" badge.

**To hide the section entirely:** toggle **Show this section** off.

## 8. Updating contact info, email, social links

**Site Settings → General Site Info** — edit email, LinkedIn, ResearchGate, UNFPA profile, etc.

## 9. Common questions

**How long until my changes appear live?**
1–2 minutes. The site automatically rebuilds when you click Publish.

**I made a mistake — can I undo?**
Yes. Every change is tracked. Contact your developer (Rafi) and any previous version can be restored.

**Can I work from my phone?**
Yes. The admin panel works on mobile, including image uploads from your camera roll.

**Will my draft be visible to anyone before I publish?**
No. Drafts are only visible to you when logged in.

**What if I want a totally new section on the site?**
Contact Rafi — adding a new chapter requires code changes.
