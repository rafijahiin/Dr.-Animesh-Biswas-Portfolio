# How to publish — Dr. Animesh Biswas

A non-technical guide for posting writing, uploading photos, and managing the site.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **`https://dranimeshportfolio.netlify.app/admin/`**.
2. Click **Login with Netlify Identity**.
3. Enter the email + password from your invitation.

The browser remembers you — log in once per device.

---

## 2. What you can edit

The admin has these sections in the left sidebar:

| Section | Where it appears on the site |
|---|---|
| **Field Notes (Professional)** | Essays/dispatches on policy, MPDSR, SRH. Homepage preview + `/field-notes` page |
| **Stories & Poems (Personal)** | Creative writing in English or Bangla. Homepage preview + `/stories` page |
| **Photo Gallery** | Homepage Photography section (categorized) |
| **Selected Publications** | `/publications` page |
| **Advisory Areas** | Homepage Advisory cards |
| **Social Links** | Homepage Socials grid + nav icons |
| **Site Settings** | Hero text, photos, section headings, contact info, stats numbers |

---

## 3. Writing a Field Note (professional essay)

1. **Field Notes (Professional) → New Field Note**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**
   - **Published date**
   - **Category badge** — Maternal Health / SRH Policy / MPDSR / Commentary / etc.
   - **Cover image** (optional — drag/upload a photo)
   - **Summary** — 1–3 sentences shown on the homepage card.
   - **Featured** — toggle ON for one essay to highlight.
   - **Draft** — leave ON while writing; turn OFF when ready to publish.
   - **Tags** — optional keywords.
   - **Body** — write the essay. Use the formatting toolbar for headings, bold, italics, lists, quotes, images, links.
3. Click **Save** → **Publish**. The site rebuilds itself in ~90 seconds.

---

## 4. Writing a Story or Poem (personal)

1. **Stories & Poems (Personal) → New Story / Poem**.
2. Same form as Field Notes, with these extras:
   - **Language** — pick **English** or **বাংলা (Bengali)**. The Bengali font loads automatically and the post page renders in Bengali typography.
   - **Kind** — Story / Poem / Reflection (shown as a badge).
3. Click **Save → Publish**.

Stories appear on the homepage (latest 3 featured) and on the `/stories` page (all of them).

---

## 5. Uploading photos to the gallery

1. **Photo Gallery → New Photo**.
2. **Photo** — drag/drop or click to upload.
3. **Category** — Field / Conferences / Community / Personal.
4. **Caption, Location, Date** — optional but helps with browsing.
5. **Display order** — lower numbers appear first within their category.
6. **Featured** — toggle ON for a wider tile.
7. **Save → Publish**.

Visitors can filter by category and click any photo to enlarge it.

---

## 6. Adding advisory areas (services cards)

The homepage shows your practice areas as cards. Currently 4 are seeded. To change them:

1. **Advisory Areas → New Advisory Card** (or edit an existing one).
2. **Title, Short description, Bullet points** (optional list).
3. **Icon** — pick from heart / shield / globe / spark / pulse / leaf / compass.
4. **Display order** — lower = appears first.

You can add 3, 5, 6 cards — whatever feels right.

---

## 7. Adding/editing social links

1. **Social Links → New Social Link**.
2. **Platform name** (e.g. "Twitter", "Threads").
3. **URL** (full link including https://).
4. **Icon** — pick from linkedin / youtube / researchgate / x / instagram / github / email / globe.
5. **Call-to-action text** — short text under the icon ("Watch on AniTap").

They appear in the homepage Socials grid AND as small icons in the nav.

---

## 8. Updating section headings, taglines, hero text

Everything visible on the site is editable.

**Site Settings → General Site Info & Section Text**

Here you can change:
- Hero greeting, name, role tagline, both button labels
- Hero photo (vertical/portrait, 4:5 ratio recommended)
- Portrait photo (small, round — used in My Story preview and About page)
- Short bio (1-2 paragraphs shown on homepage)
- Full bio (the longer page at `/about`)
- All section titles ("My Story", "Advisory Areas", "Photography", etc.)
- Contact section heading
- Footer copyright text

Save → Publish → live in ~90 seconds.

---

## 9. Updating stats numbers

**Site Settings → Numbers (Stats block)**

Edit publications count, years, countries, advisory years. They appear in the Stats block under the hero. Save → Publish.

## 11. The "Request bibliography" button

You have two options:

- **Default:** opens a new email to you with subject "Bibliography request".
- **Upload a PDF:** Site Settings → Bibliography PDF → upload your full bibliography. The button automatically becomes a direct download.

## 12. Common questions

**How long until my changes appear live?**
~90 seconds. Site rebuilds automatically when you click Publish.

**I made a mistake — can I undo?**
Every change is tracked. Contact Rafi to restore any previous version.

**Can I work from my phone?**
Yes. Admin and uploads work on mobile.

**My draft — visible to anyone before I publish?**
No. Drafts only show when you're logged in.
