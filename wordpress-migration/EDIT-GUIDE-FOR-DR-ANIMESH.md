## 3. How to replace a photo (most important)

Every page has photo spots. They look like cream-coloured boxes with text saying things like **"📷 At UNFPA Bangladesh, on assignment"** with a caption below saying **"↑ Replace with a photo of you at work — field, office, or event"**.

These are **placeholder images** waiting for your real photos.

### Step-by-step (takes 30 seconds per photo)

1. Click **Pages** in the left menu → pick the page (e.g., "Work" or "About Me") → click the page name.
2. **Scroll down to find the placeholder photo** you want to change.
3. **Click directly on the placeholder photo image**. A toolbar appears at the top of the image.
4. In the toolbar, click **"Replace"** (or the three dots ⋮ → Replace).
5. Click **"Upload"** → pick the photo from your computer.
6. Wait a few seconds for upload.
7. **Click "Update"** (the blue button at the top right of the page).
8. Done. Your photo is now on the live site.

### Tips

- **Best photo size**: 1200 × 675 pixels (or bigger). The site auto-resizes.
- **File types**: JPG, PNG, WebP.
- **Don't worry about file size** — WordPress optimizes everything.
- **Keep originals on your computer** — never delete your only copy.
- **Edit the caption too**: below the photo there's a small "Replace with..." caption. Click on it and type your own (e.g., "Cox's Bazar, 2023").

### Where you'll find placeholder photos

| **About Me** | 1 main portrait (vertical) |

## 4. How to write a new blog post

### To write a story, poem, travel log, or field note

1. Click **Posts** in the left menu.
2. Click the black **Add New Post** button at the top.
3. Type your **title** in the big top box.
4. Click below it and **start typing**.

### Adding pictures inside your story

- Click the **+** button (left or above the cursor) → **Image** → upload.
- For a YouTube video: paste the YouTube link on its own line. WordPress auto-embeds.

### Bengali / English

Just type. The site handles both languages automatically.

### Categories — choose where the post appears

On the **right side panel**, find the **Categories** box:
- ✅ **Travel Blog** — for travel articles (appear on the Travel page)
- ✅ **Stories** — for short stories (appear on Stories & Blog page)
- ✅ **Poems** — for poetry

Tick the right one.

### Featured image (the preview photo)

On the right side, scroll down to **Featured Image** → click **Set featured image** → upload.

### Publishing vs draft

- Click **Publish** (top right) to go live.
- Click **Save Draft** to save without publishing.

## 5. How to upload photos to the gallery

The **Photography** page has **8 empty gallery boxes**:
1. Public Health
2. People & Communities
3. Nature & Landscapes
4. Portraits
5. Flowers & Plants
6. Foods & Nutrition
7. Field Works & Activities
8. Award Winning Photos

When empty, each one shows a peach-colored placeholder saying **"📷 Photos coming soon"**.

### To fill a gallery

1. Click **Pages** → **Photography** → click to open.
2. Scroll down to the empty gallery for the category you want.
3. **Click the empty gallery box**.
4. Click **"Add Images"** (or **Upload**).
5. Pick photos from your computer (hold Ctrl/Cmd to select many at once).
6. Photos appear as a grid. **Drag** to reorder. Click any photo to add a caption.
7. **Click Update** (top right).

Your photos appear on the live page within 2 seconds.

### Tips

- Drop **5–30 photos per category** for a strong gallery.
- Smaller is fine — WordPress auto-resizes.
- Want to add more photos later? Just open the Photography page again, click the gallery, click "+" → Add Images.

## 6. How to change text on any page

Almost every word on the website can be edited.

1. **Pages** → click the page.
2. **Click directly on the text** you want to change.
3. Just type — like Google Docs or Microsoft Word.
4. **Bold** = press Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on Mac). *Italic* = Ctrl+I.
5. Click **Update** when done.

To make something a heading: highlight the text → in the toolbar that appears, click the small **¶** dropdown → pick **Heading**.

## 7. How to add a new section / box / number on any page

You have **Kadence Blocks** installed — a powerful set of pre-designed sections you can drop in.

### To add a new section

1. **Edit a page**.
2. Click the **+** button (left side or wherever you want the new section).
3. Type what you want to add. Examples:
   - **Number Counter** — for an animated stat number (like "100+ Projects")
   - **Info Box** — for a card with icon + heading + description (like the advisory areas)
   - **Row Layout** — for a custom section with colored background
   - **Accordion** — for an expanding FAQ section
   - **Icon List** — for a bulleted list with custom icons
   - **Testimonial** — for a quote with photo + name
4. Click the block you want.
5. Fill in the fields (visually — click each part and type).
6. Click **Update**.

This is the best way to **add new things** to the site without asking Rafi.

## 9. Common questions

### Q: I broke something! What do I do?

Don't panic.

1. **Just edited and looks wrong?** Press **Ctrl+Z** (Undo) at the top of the editor.
2. **Already saved?** Open the page → on the right side panel, click **Revisions** → pick an older version → restore.
3. **Still confused?** **Call Rafi** — don't try to fix more.

### Q: I see "Block contains unexpected or invalid content" with an "Attempt recovery" button.

Just click **"Attempt recovery"**. WordPress fixes it automatically. Then **Update**.

### Q: I see "Convert to blocks" on a block.

Same — click it. Then **Update**.

### Q: I see numbers like 19+, 14+, 35+ on the homepage — how do I change them?

Those are special design elements. To change them: **Edit the Home page**, scroll down to the navy stats strip, find the number, click on it. If clicking doesn't work to edit, **Rafi can change them** in 30 seconds — just send him a message saying "Change 19+ to 20+".

### Q: How long until my changes appear on the live site?

Within 5 seconds of clicking **Update** or **Publish**. Refresh the live site to see.

### Q: Can I work from my phone?

Yes — `https://animeshbiswas.com/wp-admin/` works on phone too. For writing long posts, a computer is easier.

### Q: My photo isn't uploading.

- File might be too big (over 20 MB). Shrink it first (any phone app can do this).
- Internet might be slow — wait.
- File should be `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp`. PDF / .heic won't work.

### Q: How do I log out?

Top right corner → hover your name → **Log Out**.

### Q: I forgot my password.

On login page, click **Lost your password?** → enter your email → check inbox.

### Q: Can someone else log in to edit?

Only you and Rafi have admin access right now. To invite someone else:

1. **Users → Add New**.
2. Fill in name, email, password.
3. **Role**: choose **Editor** (can write/edit but can't break things).
4. Click **Add New User**.

> Don't give anyone "Administrator" access unless you fully trust them.

### Q: How do I edit the site title or main colors?

**Appearance → Customize** → use the menu on the left side.

### Q: Where do new blog posts appear?

Depends on the category:
- **Travel Blog** → on the Travel page
- **Stories** or **Poems** → on the Stories & Blog page
- **Field Notes** category → on the Travel page
