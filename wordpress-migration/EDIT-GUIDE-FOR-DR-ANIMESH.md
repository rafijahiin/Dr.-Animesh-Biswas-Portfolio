## 3. How to change a photo on the website

Every page has spots where you can put your own photos. They look like this:

```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│           ↑             │
│    Upload Portrait      │
│    Photo                │
│                         │
│ Edit page → Replace     │
│ this block with an      │
│ image (4:5 portrait)    │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

That orange dashed box is **where you upload a photo**. Here's how:

### Step-by-step

1. Click **Pages** in the left menu.
2. Find the page you want to add a photo to (for example, **Home** or **About Me**).
3. Click its name to open it.
4. You'll see the page with all its content blocks.
5. **Click directly on the orange dashed box** that says "Upload Portrait Photo".
6. A small toolbar appears at the top of that block. Click the **three dots ⋮** → **Delete**.
7. Now there's an empty spot. Click the **+ (plus)** button that appears.
8. Type "Image" in the search box and click **Image**.
9. Click **Upload**.
10. Pick the photo from your computer.
11. Wait for it to upload (5-30 seconds depending on size).
12. **Click the blue "Update" button** at the top right of the page. Your photo is now live.

### Tips for photos

- **Best size**: 1200 × 1500 pixels (taller than wide is good for portraits)
- **File type**: JPG or PNG
- **Don't worry about file size** — WordPress will optimize it
- **Don't put your only copy** — keep originals on your computer

## 4. How to write a new blog post

### To write a personal story, poem, or travel log

1. Click **Posts** in the left menu.
2. Click the black **Add New Post** button at the top.
3. Type your title in the big box at the top (where it says "Add title").
4. Click below it and start typing your story.

### Adding pictures inside your story

- To put a photo inside the story: click the **+** button on the left → **Image** → upload.
- To put a YouTube video inside the story: paste the YouTube link on its own line. WordPress automatically turns it into a video.

### Bengali / English

- Just type in either language — the site automatically displays whatever you write.

### Categories

On the **right side panel**, look for the **Categories** box:
- ✅ **Travel Blog** — for travel articles
- ✅ **Stories** — for short stories
- ✅ **Poems** — for poetry

Tick the right one. The article will appear on the correct page automatically.

### Featured image (the photo people see in the preview card)

On the right side, scroll down to **Featured Image** → click **Set featured image** → upload or pick from library.

### Publishing

When you're ready, click the blue **Publish** button (top right). 

> If you want to **save and come back later without publishing**, click the small "Save Draft" link instead. It stays hidden from visitors until you click Publish.

## 5. How to upload photos to the gallery

The **Photography** page has 7 categories:
- Public Health
- People & Communities
- Nature & Landscapes
- Portraits
- Flowers & Plants
- Foods & Nutrition
- Field Works & Activities

### To add photos to a category

1. Click **Modula** in the left menu.
2. If you don't see a gallery for that category yet, click **Add New** (top button).
3. Name the gallery (e.g., "Public Health" or "Nepal Flowers").
4. Click **Add Images** → upload from your computer (you can select many at once).
5. After upload, you can drag photos to reorder them.
6. Each photo can have a small caption — click the photo and type.
7. Click the blue **Save Gallery** button.
8. Copy the **shortcode** that appears (looks like `[modula id="5"]`).
9. Now open **Pages** → **Photography** → find the right section heading → paste the shortcode under it.
10. Click **Update**.

The photos appear on the live site within a few seconds.

## 6. How to change text on any page

Almost every word on the website can be edited. To change anything:

1. **Pages** → click the page you want to change.
2. Click on the text you want to edit (a cursor appears).
3. Just type — like Microsoft Word.
4. **Bold** = press Ctrl+B (or Cmd+B on Mac). *Italic* = Ctrl+I.
5. To make a heading: highlight the text and click the **Paragraph** dropdown at the top, choose **Heading**.
6. When done, click the blue **Update** button (top right).

### Special text trick — orange color

Want to highlight a word in orange (like the "Humanity" word on the home page)?

1. Highlight the word.
2. Click the small **down arrow ▼** in the toolbar.
3. Choose **Inline image** → no, choose **Highlight** or **Color**.
4. Pick the orange color.

If this is too tricky, just leave a note and Rafi will style it.

## 8. How to save without making it public

Working on something that isn't ready yet? Don't worry, you can hide it.

For posts/articles:
- On the right side panel, change **Status** from "Published" to "Draft".
- Or just click **Save Draft** instead of **Publish**.

For pages:
- Same — on the right side, change Status to **Draft**.

You can come back and finish anytime by going to Posts/Pages and clicking the article again.

## 9. Common questions

### Q: I broke something! What do I do?

Don't panic. Almost nothing is permanent.

1. If you just edited a page and it looks wrong: at the very top of the editor, click the **↶ Undo** arrow (or press Ctrl+Z).
2. If you already saved: click the **Revisions** link on the right panel — WordPress remembers EVERY version. Pick an older one and restore it.
3. If still confused: **Call Rafi**. Don't try to fix more — it'll just get worse.

### Q: How long until my changes appear on the live site?

Instantly. As soon as you click **Update** or **Publish**, the change is live within 5 seconds. Refresh the public site to see it.

### Q: Can I work from my phone?

Yes. Open the same `/wp-admin/` address on your phone. Everything works, but it's easier on a computer for long writing.

### Q: My photo isn't uploading.

- The file might be too big (over 20 MB). Try shrinking it first.
- Or your internet might be slow — wait a bit.
- Or the file isn't a normal image — it should be `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp`.

### Q: How do I log out?

Top right corner of admin → hover over your name → **Log Out**.

### Q: I forgot my password.

On the login page, click **Lost your password?** and follow the email. If you don't get the email, call Rafi.

### Q: Can someone else log in as me and edit?

Only people with admin accounts can edit. Right now only **you** and **Rafi** have access.

### Q: How do I add a new admin (give someone else editing access)?

1. Left menu → **Users** → **Add New**.
2. Fill in their name, email, password.
3. **Role**: choose **Editor** (can write/edit but can't break settings) or **Administrator** (full power).
4. Click **Add New User**.

> **Be careful** giving Administrator access — only to people you fully trust.
