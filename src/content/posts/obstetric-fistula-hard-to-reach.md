Obstetric fistula remains one of the most devastating and least visible maternal health injuries in the world. Behind the clinical name is a woman — often young, often poor, often in a place a clinic has never quite reached.

## The structural causes

The conditions that produce fistula are rarely about a single failed delivery. They are about the long chain of decisions before it: whether she could finish school, whether the road to the nearest facility was passable, whether the facility had a trained provider on the night her labor stalled.

## What the field has taught us

Over a decade of working in hard-to-reach areas of Bangladesh, three things have become clear:

1. **Surveillance precedes response.** You cannot fix what you do not count.
2. **Community trust is infrastructure.** It is built slowly and lost quickly.
3. **Cash, where appropriate, dignifies care.** Vouchers and direct transfers can do what clinics alone cannot.
