A short biography

Dr. Animesh Biswas is a global health practitioner at UNFPA Bangladesh, working at the difficult edges of reproductive health — where policy meets crisis, and where systems must be built before they can be measured.

He pioneered the use of Cash and Voucher Assistance for Sexual and Reproductive Health outcomes in humanitarian emergencies — a model now studied, validated and replicated across multiple countries. The work began in the Rohingya response in Cox's Bazar and has since travelled.

A standing member of the WHO Global MPDSR Technical Working Group for over five years, he has contributed case studies to WHO's 2024 global report and advised on the international guidance for cash-based programming in SRH.
